Privacy in Buzzy ABC Garden

Buzzy ABC Garden is made for children aged 3–6. It is built to collect nothing about them.

What we don't collect

No accounts, names, photos, voice recordings, location, device IDs or typed text. The game never uses the microphone or camera.

No ads, no tracking

There are no ads, no analytics and no third-party services. Everything the game needs is loaded from this website.

What stays on this device

The game remembers which flowers have bloomed, which language you chose, the tracing difficulty and the volume. This is stored only in this browser on this device. Nobody else can see it. You can erase it any time with "Reset progress" in the grown-ups area, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Links

The only links that leave the game are in the grown-ups area, behind a grown-up check.
